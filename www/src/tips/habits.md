# Habits

![Habits](/assets/habits-light.svg)

Habits are the small things you want to do every day — drink water, stretch,
read a chapter. Each habit has an emoji, a name, how many times a day you want
to do it, and which days of the week it counts.

## Using habits

Your habits appear as a row of emoji rings above the task list on the
[Day](/tips/day) and [Week](/tips/week) views, and in the **Review** step of
your evening ritual. Only habits scheduled for that day show up.

Tap a ring to log one step. The ring fills as you go, and turns solid with a
check mark once you hit your goal. A habit you do once a day is a simple
check-off. Tapped one too many times? Tapping a finished ring resets it to zero.

You can log today and catch up on the past 30 days. Future days are shown faded
and can't be tapped yet.

### Widgets

On iPhone and iPad, add the **Habits** widget to your home screen to see today's
habits at a glance. Tap a ring to log a step without opening Dexter — it syncs
to your other devices the next time you open the app.

## Settings

Open **Settings** and choose **Habits** to manage your habits.

### Turning habits on and off

- **Enabled**: Habit rings appear on your days, weeks, and rituals
- **Disabled**: Habits are hidden everywhere

### Adding habits

Tap <i class="ph-bold ph-plus"></i> to create a habit. Pick an emoji, give it a
name, set how many **times per day** you want to do it, and choose the **days**
it's active.

### Editing habits

Tap a habit to change it. New settings apply from today onward — past days keep
the progress you logged.

### Pausing habits

Use the pause button beside a habit to take a break from it. Paused habits
disappear from your rings until you resume them.

### Archiving habits

Open a habit and use **Archive** to retire it. Archiving hides the habit but
keeps its history.
